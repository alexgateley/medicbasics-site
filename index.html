Questions

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Every question and scenario is original, written to the published content outlines, then checked for accuracy and for the "tells" that let test-wise candidates guess. Medic Basics is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with the National Registry or the IBSC.

How do the adaptive mock exams work?

Each answer changes the difficulty of the next item, as computerized adaptive testing does on the National Registry exams. The mock stops once it is confident your ability is above or below the passing standard, or at the maximum number of items.

Do I need an account? Where is my progress kept?

No account. Your progress stays in your browser on this device, and you can download a backup file and restore it on another device. The only data sent is anonymous usage analytics, which you can turn off in Settings → Your Data.

When are the iPhone and Android apps coming?

Both are coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Until then, the web app has everything and works in any browser, including on your phone.

Who makes Medic Basics?

A paramedic and ICU nurse educator (NRP, BSN, CCRN, CEN), the maker of ICU Basics and RN Basics.

I think a question is wrong.

Tap "Report a problem with this question" under any explanation. It opens an email with the question already filled in, and every report is reviewed.
